Authentication: Who are you? Example: Are you a logged-in user?
Authorization: What are you allowed to do? Example: Are you allowed to delete another user's account?



How will we implement RBAC?
We'll add a role field to our existing User schema.
For example:
role: {
    type: String,
    enum: ["user", "admin"],
    default: "user"
}
We'll create authorization middleware that checks the user's role before allowing access to certain APIs.
